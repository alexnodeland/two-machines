# Listen

An annotated curriculum, ordered for learning rather than by date: each record is
here because it teaches one thing the previous one set up. One sentence per record
on what to listen _for_; every link goes out to a legal stream or the label's own
shop. **No audio is hosted here, ever** — this page is a map, not a jukebox.

1. **Fripp & Eno — [_(No Pussyfooting)_](https://open.spotify.com/album/3gHpX5wJn0kzAXJvwORG6u)** (1973).
   The system meeting a guitar for the first time: listen for how few gestures
   "The Heavenly Music Corporation" is actually made of, and how long each one
   stays useful.
2. **Brian Eno — [_Discreet Music_](https://open.spotify.com/album/7b67MKOwyj7mIcEVnoJSOG)** (1975).
   The diagram from [chapter 1](/machine/what-it-is/) running almost by itself:
   listen for the system as composer, the performer nearly absent.
3. **Robert Fripp — [_Let the Power Fall_](https://dgmlive.com/products/let-the-power-fall-CD)** (1981).
   Pure Frippertronics, documented: listen for how each piece manages its decay —
   when he lets the loop thin out, and when he refuses to.
4. **Robert Fripp — [_Exposure_](https://open.spotify.com/album/58HHtTfwSBN2GUa3l1ZbP3)** (1979).
   Applied mode inside a song album: listen for loops working as _texture under_
   rather than _subject of_, especially against the vocal tracks.
5. **Daryl Hall — [_Sacred Songs_](https://open.spotify.com/album/2mwf6VYgVtpMWmviACI5tB)** (1980).
   Per Tamm the first recorded Frippertronics: listen to the Side One suite for
   the loops threading a pop record without asking permission.
6. **David Bowie — [_"Heroes"_](https://open.spotify.com/album/4I5zzKYd2SKDgZ9DRf5LVk)** (1977).
   The title track's guitar is one player, distance, and feedback: listen for how
   the sustained line sits at the edge of controllable.
7. **The Roches — [_The Roches_](https://open.spotify.com/album/2gLirULn7YwSGINsyHSxxR)** (1979).
   Fripp producing ("audio vérité") and playing on an acoustic trio record:
   listen for restraint as a production value, and the one solo that isn't
   restrained at all.
8. **David Sylvian — [_Gone to Earth_](https://open.spotify.com/album/3CRipGCmcoBTKdadiYIMOc)** (1986).
   The sustained-guitar vocabulary a decade on: listen for how long tones carry
   harmony the way the tape once did.
9. **Fripp & Eno — [_The Equatorial Stars_](https://open.spotify.com/album/25us3iSYDeZ7DjV458Uems)** (2004).
   The 1973 duo with thirty more years of listening: compare its patience directly
   with _(No Pussyfooting)_.
10. **Robert Fripp — [_Music for Quiet Moments_](https://dgmlive.com/products/music-for-quiet-moments)** (2020–21).
    Soundscapes, one a week for a year: listen for the practice — commit, listen,
    add, wait — surviving every change of hardware.
11. **King Crimson — [_Discipline_](https://open.spotify.com/album/7KxUQCCvE0edGiqBS6ywEd)** (1981).
    The interlock chapter's numbers on record: listen for the title track's two
    guitars drifting apart in your attention and snapping back — you now know
    exactly when.
12. **The League of Crafty Guitarists — [_Live IV — Argentina 2007_](https://theleagueofcraftyguitarists.bandcamp.com/album/live-iv-argentina-2007-2021-remaster)** (2021 remaster).
    Circulations in the wild: listen for lines no single player is playing. (The
    1986 _Live!_ album has no official stream we could verify; this, from the
    ensemble's own Bandcamp, is the canonical legally-streaming document.)

If one listening assignment must stand for all of it: play the _Discipline_ title
track immediately after clapping five-against-seven with [Part I](/two-cycles/)'s
engine, and notice that you can now hear the seams.
