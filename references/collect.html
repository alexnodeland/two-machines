Eno, Discreet Music — the liner schematic

blocks ch. 1

eno-discreet-music-sleeve

The 1975 sleeve carries Eno's own diagram of the two-machine feedback loop. The site's hero diagram descends from it, and we've committed to drawing an honest descendant rather than reconstructing one from memory — so we need to see the real thing.

A photo of the sleeve is fine. A good scan is better.

inbox/eno-discreet-music-sleeve.jpg

We reproduce nothing — we draw our own version in our own visual language and say what it descends from.

Michigan Daily, 14 June 1979, p. 7

blocks ch. 3.2

michigan-daily-1979

“Second Chance rips out” by Keith Tosolt — a review of the Ann Arbor Frippertronics show. It contains the passage the spectral-harmony section rests on, plus a useful line about Fripp deliberately avoiding hard left/right separation.

Open the page → Use Download Text in its Page Options panel, or just Save Page As.

inbox/michigan-daily-1979.html

A Cloudflare managed challenge defeated curl, headless Chromium, headless Chrome, headful Chrome with a persistent profile, and the direct download endpoint. It loads fine in your normal browser. Don't let me spend more time automating it.

Dublin keynote, August 2025

ch. 7, 8, Part IV

dublin-keynote-2025

Fripp's keynote to the Alexander Technique Congress, seven parts on YouTube, uncaptioned. Source of the counting-and-clapping exercise the Cycles engine models. What's needed first is just the seven video IDs — transcription after that.

Paste the URLs anywhere I'll see them; I'll add them to the manifest.

The Midnight Special, 5 October 1979

ch. 1, Part IV

midnight-special-1979

The only footage of Fripp explaining the rig on camera — about four minutes, in which he dedicates the performance to the proposition of hazard and admits he doesn't know what will happen either. The natural “watch this first” artifact.

No stable URL found. It circulates via Dangerous Minds and Synthtopia write-ups. A working link is all we need — we'd link out, never rehost.

Ron Gaskin interview, 1979

ch. 1

gaskin-1979

Reportedly the clearest mechanical description Fripp ever gave of the signal path — the recorded signal passing along the tape to the second machine, played back, recorded again. We only have it via secondary quotation.

Never located. Worth trying Elephant Talk's wider archive and 1979 fanzine scans if you have a route in.

Guitar Craft Monograph No. 3, 1987

ch. 3.2

gc-monograph-3

Out-of-print poster. Contains the harmonic-series statement — one fundamental, then two, then a triad and affairs stand a good chance of getting severely out of hand — which is the theoretical justification for “avoid mud”.

Reference only, never reproduced. An image is for us to read from; any aphorism quoted counts against the site-wide cap of four.
