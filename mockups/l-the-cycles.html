Our framing, not Fripp's

Two cycles of different length, and what happens when they realign.

A tape delay is a fixed cycle running against your phrase length. Fripp's fifteen runs against Belew's fourteen. A room counts five claps against seven. The claim of this site is that these are one object at three scales — and that the difference between the two ways they can fail to line up is the thing nobody writes down.
