Our framing, not Fripp's

A delay line is a perpetual canon you cannot retune

Everything you play comes back at exactly T, then 2T, then 3T. That is a canon at the unison — and the one free parameter a composer normally has, the interval between voices, is set by where the furniture is and cannot be changed mid-piece.

So the only variable left to you is phrase length. Set it to divide T and you are writing a canon in the ordinary sense. Set it to anything else and you get the thing Riley named more accurately than anyone since: a Time Lag Accumulator.
