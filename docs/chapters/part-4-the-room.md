# Part IV · The Room

## One idea

Listening is an act of attention; hearing is passive — and the whole performance
practice (the venues, the scale, the economics) follows from taking that seriously.

## What the reader can do afterwards

State the listening/hearing distinction in Fripp's terms, and explain why a man with a
hit-making band played record shops and pizzerias instead.

## Outline

Short by design; no interactive. **Fripp's own sentences do the work** — this page is
built from quotation, which is why it is written **late** in the
[§7 writing order](../architecture/content-methodology.md#7-writing-order): the ~50-word
per-source and four-aphorism budgets are whole-site constraints, and Part IV is drafted
only once we know what the chapters have already spent.

1. **Listening vs. hearing.** The one verbatim quotation this page must carry — the
   Tooley passage, one of the two sanctioned long quotations
   ([Content methodology §4](../architecture/content-methodology.md#quotation-budget)):
   *"A person who listens exerts an act of attention. A person who hears is a passive
   listener, if you like. A passive hearer…"* Blockquote, attributed inline, linked.
2. **The proposition of hazard.** Performance as a place where something real is at
   stake for both parties. Reported in Fripp's terms; exact sourcing to be pinned
   during drafting (candidates: Tooley, Midnight Special, Dublin keynote) — no
   paraphrase ships without a source entry.
3. **Record shops, pizzerias and a planetarium.** The 1979 Frippertronics tour
   (April–August 1979, per Tamm), so nobody is owed the hits. The *Michigan Daily*
   "demystifying experience" line — reordering preconceptions about the rock star and
   the rock concert — belongs here.
4. **The small, mobile, intelligent unit.** As much a business-model argument as a
   musical one: the phrase (Tamm: "the Frippism par excellence of the late 1970s")
   ties the aesthetic to the economics of the dinosaur-band collapse. One paragraph;
   the argument, not the biography.

## Interactive

None. The prose carries it (page spec). This is the one Part where an instrument would
be a distraction: the subject is attention itself.

## Sources

  wiki) — the listening/hearing passage verbatim; the richest single document.
- **The Midnight Special**, taped 5 Oct 1979 — the only on-camera rig explanation;
  status ⚠️ *locate* in the [bibliography](../architecture/bibliography.md#1-primary--fripps-own-words).
- **Tamm 1990** — tour dates, the small-mobile-intelligent-unit phrase and its framing.
- ***Michigan Daily***, 14 June 1979 — the "demystifying experience" aim.
- **Dublin keynote 2025** — needs transcription before anything from it is quoted
  (bibliography §10).

  not asserted, until then.

  economic; Fripp's own account makes them both, and the page keeps both.
