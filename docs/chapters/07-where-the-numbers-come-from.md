# Chapter 7 · Where the numbers come from

## One idea

Fripp's interest in cyclic number is doctrinally sourced, not decorative.

## What the reader can do afterwards

Say where the numbers come from — Bennett at Sherborne House, 1975–76; Bennett a
student of Gurdjieff — and separate the one true acoustic fact underneath the Law of
Seven, which they have now *heard*, from the cosmology built on top of it, which they
have been *told about*, with the teller named.

## Outline

Short and cool-headed — the shortest chapter in the Discipline half, and the register
is fixed before a word is drafted: report, don't endorse; explain, don't sneer; get
back to the music (Content methodology §1).

1. **Sherborne House, 1975–76.** Biographical fact, sourced to Tamm: Fripp's withdrawal
   and the ten-month course at Bennett's International Academy for Continuous Education
   at Sherborne. Bennett was a student of Gurdjieff. Two sentences, dated, done.
2. **What the doctrine says** — via Robertson's summaries, never Gurdjieff directly
   (ADR-034). The Law of Seven (*Heptaparaparshinokh*, the Law of Octaves): every
   process unfolds like an ascending octave and deflects at the octave's two gaps
   unless a shock is applied; the named intervals are *stopinders*. The Law of Three.
   All of it in reported speech throughout — what Gurdjieff taught, not what is so.
3. **The true part.** The diatonic octave really does step by a semitone at mi→fa and
   si→do while every other step is a tone. The interactive demonstrates this; the
   prose only names it: Gurdjieff built a cosmology on a real property of the scale,
   and that is why an honest interactive is possible here at all (ADR-044).
4. **The open question, stated as open.** Whether the seven-based clapping patterns
   are doctrinal or a convenient prime is not known (v2 §5; the hedged-claims list in
   Content methodology §2). One sentence on this page says so. Chapter 8 inherits the
   hedge and does not reopen it.
5. **Back to the music.** "Heptaparaparshinokh" is the A-side of a 1980 League of
   Gentlemen single, and its B-side, "Marriagemuzic", is a standalone Pure
   Frippertronics piece — both halves of this site on one piece of vinyl. The fact
   worth the chapter; end on it and link out to `/listen`.

## Claims requiring the editorial mark

None, by design. The chapter's discipline is that every sentence is one of: sourced
fact (Tamm, the release), reported doctrine (Robertson, in reported speech), or
demonstrated acoustics (the interactive). The one idea is Robertson's argument and is
attributed to him rather than marked as ours. A drafted sentence that turns out to be
neither sourceable nor attributable is cut, not marked.

## Claims that stay hedged

- Whether the seven-based clapping patterns are doctrinal or a convenient prime —
  stated as open on this page, in one sentence, and left open.
- How far the doctrine reaches into the music (the *Discipline* meters, the
  seven-note groupings) — never asserted beyond what Robertson argues, always
  attributed to him.
- What Fripp privately believes. The chapter reports what he studied, where, and when
  — nothing further.

## Rights notes

- **Gurdjieff is never quoted directly** (ADR-034 — copyright varies by edition and
  jurisdiction; Robertson's summaries carry the doctrine).
- Quotation budget applies to Robertson as to everyone: ≤~50 continuous words;
  paraphrase mostly, attribute always.
- No aphorism is spent here; the site-wide cap of four (ADR-030) is untouched.
- No hosted audio (ADR-032) — "Marriagemuzic" is a `/listen` link to a legal stream.
- The interactive's tones are generic: a scale in a generic timbre, no recording,
  nothing derivative.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reader can hear *and* see that mi→fa and si→do are half the step of the others
      — the true part is demonstrated, not narrated.
- [ ] The doctrinal layer appears only behind the explicit toggle, visually distinct,
      phrased throughout as reported doctrine.
- [ ] Nothing on the page is a playable seven-cycle.
- [ ] The clapping-pattern question is stated as open, and the interactive does not
      answer it by implication.
- [ ] Any enneagram appears only inside the Law of Three passage, labelled as doctrine.
- [ ] Every factual claim resolves to `/sources` in one click; no editorial mark
      appears, because no editorial claim does.
- [ ] The chapter ends on the single and gets back to the music.
- [ ] Register check on the finished draft: no endorsement, no sneer.
- [ ] The chapter makes its point with audio disabled (the visual scale carries the
      two gaps).
